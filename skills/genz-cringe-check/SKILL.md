# Cringe check — useful criticism without taste pretending to be evidence

Read `../genzfy/references/anti-patterns.md`. For a standalone request, use the supplied copy and
ask only for missing context that changes the judgment: venue, speaker, audience, channel or
intended joke. Do not demand a profile. An imagined 22-year-old and the owner's comfort are not
customer research. Age does not determine taste or permission to use humor.

## Distinguish the issue

- **Taste or brand fit:** subjective. Explain the likely mismatch and uncertainty; deliberate slang,
  niche enthusiasm, older-owner role reversal and plain descriptions can all work in context.
- **Factual accuracy:** verify prices, claims, dates and facts. Never invent a venue history or sales
  count to make the rewrite sound specific.
- **Respect/context:** consider stereotyping, shame, exclusion and how the asset reads alone.
  Sincerity is not a defense for disrespect; a customer can choose not to participate.
- **Privacy/rights/disclosure:** consent, identifying customers/staff, music/image rights and paid
  endorsements require checks, not just a better tone.
- **Operational fit:** the offer needs capacity, contribution and clear conditions, not only a joke.

## Verdicts

**ships:** no concrete issue established in the supplied context. Say why briefly, and mention any
material unverified fact. It means usable draft, not proven performance or authorization to post.

**fix:** repair a specific issue. Name its category and relevant anti-pattern ID where applicable;
provide a rewrite that preserves the intent and facts. Label placeholders if facts are missing.

**kill:** a serious unrepairable false claim, disrespect/privacy risk or unviable premise. Explain
what cannot be repaired and offer a safer alternative. Do not kill a creative category because a
survey or the reviewer dislikes it. Missing evidence can require holding for a check, not a veto.

Keep the output short:

```markdown
**fix — factual accuracy / A12**
“Everyone's favorite” is unsupported by the information supplied.
**Instead:** “Your lunch break, sorted: <verified dish and total price>.”
Confirm the dish and price before posting. This is a draft, not evidence that it will convert.
```

For a disputed taste question, suggest a small, voluntary check with intended customers, asking
what they understand and whether it fits rather than leading them toward approval. Keep commercial
measurement separate from laughter or likes. Unknown and no change needed are valid outcomes.
If the owner chooses a survivable creative risk, state the uncertainty once and respect that choice;
do not relax factual, consent or safety requirements. There is no universal slang expiry date or
rule that sincere self-expression can never be cringe.
