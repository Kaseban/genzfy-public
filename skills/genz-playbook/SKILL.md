# Genz playbook — a small, defensible plan

Read the profile, audit, `../genzfy/references/patterns.md`,
`../genzfy/references/claim-ledger.md` and `../genzfy/references/anti-patterns.md`.
If a broad request has no diagnosis, use `genz-audit`; do not require a whole audit for a narrowly
specified corrective action. Never repeat a failed idea without explaining what will differ.

## Choose a move only when it earns its cost

Separate the research finding, venue hypothesis and falsifying observation. State why this venue
might benefit, not “why it works” as a guarantee. Alternatives include gathering a missing
baseline, fixing a factual error, maintaining the current practice, or no change recommended.

Estimate contribution, not revenue alone: net sales excluding pass-through taxes less product,
packaging, payment/channel
fees, refunds, variable labor and delivery/support costs as relevant. Separate setup, recurring and
opportunity costs. Compare like-for-like service: direct pickup and aggregator delivery differ.
Discount uptake, new channel orders and NA sales may substitute for existing profitable purchases.
Check cannibalization, displacement of full-price demand, waste, queues and capacity.

Do not call unpaid owner time free. If inputs are unknown, show the missing inputs or a clearly
labelled scenario/break-even range, and gate a rollout on measuring them. Never invent local
prices, baseline volumes, subscriptions, staff time or tools.

## Move format

```markdown
### M<n> · <concrete action or test>
**Finding:** <audit evidence/status> · **Evidence:** <pattern/claim IDs and limits>
**Hypothesis and alternative:** <why here, what else could explain the observation>
**What you do:** <specific steps within authorization>
**Cost:** <currency; setup cash, recurring cash, variable cost; estimates labelled>
**Effort:** <setup and recurring person-hours; opportunity cost>
**Who and capacity:** <owner, available time, fulfillment limits; confirm unknowns>
**Economics:** <expected/break-even contribution; substitution and displacement risk>
**Metric and baseline:** <business outcome, denominator, confirmed tool; unknown if missing>
**Time to learn:** <feasibility window and outcome/repeat window separately>
**Guardrails:** <margin, queue, waste, service, consent or accuracy stop conditions>
**Decision and falsifier:** <what would support, weaken or leave hypothesis inconclusive>
**Reversal:** <how to stop; sunk cost and irreversible consequences>
```

Keep the plan inside the actual budget and time available, not a team-size stereotype. If a move
cannot be measured proportionately, defer or choose a simpler question rather than invent a proxy
that supposedly proves profit. A proxy can test uptake, not necessarily commercial impact.

## Prioritize and review

- **Now:** up to three supported affordable actions or information checks. No minimum number and
  no mandatory zero-cost suggestions. A low-cash action can still have substantial labor cost.
- **Next:** depends on learning, funding or capacity. State the dependency.
- **Later:** capital or longer commitment, with a specific trigger and risk. Do not fully specify
  speculative projects merely to fill a section.
- **Stop or pause:** only where evidence supports doing so; name what it frees up and any downside.
  Do not automatically delete a platform, loyalty scheme or customer-content idea.

Check already-tried history, contribution, staff load, factual claims and `genz-cringe-check` for
public-facing creative. Creative taste alone does not veto a business move. Hold demand-generating
promotion when the venue cannot fulfill, the purchase path is broken, or safety/accuracy/legal-age
issues remain unresolved. Poor photos or an unvalidated visual assessment alone do not block
corrected hours, helpful service information or honest updates.

## Output and handoff

Write `genzfy-workspace/<slug>/playbook.md` with date, audit reference, budget/time limits,
Now/Next/Later/Stop (omit empty sections), the move fields above and evidence limitations. If none
is justified, say why and the smallest useful next check. Offer `genz-pilot` for one chosen
intervention. Two weeks may establish feasibility; repeat visits or seasonal effects need longer.

Use concrete actions instead of “be authentic”, “create shareable moments” or “engage with your
community”. Voluntary participation can be tested; a dependable content plan must not rely on
untested customer posting. Cost labor, consent, creator fees and rights for each production model.
