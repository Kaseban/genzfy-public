## Research-method guardrails

- Pew Research Center, May 22, 2023, [How Pew Research Center will report on generations moving forward](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/05/22/how-pew-research-center-will-report-on-generations-moving-forward/): age, life stage, historical period and cohort need careful separation; generation labels can obscure meaningful within-group differences.
- Rudolph and colleagues, [Generations and Generational Differences: Debunking Myths in Organizational Science and Practice and Paving New Paths Forward](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7471586/), 2021 journal publication: a methodological reference, not restaurant consumer evidence. Cross-sectional differences do not identify independent generational effects.
- **Practical experimental guidance (operational judgment):** state the hypothesis, audience, primary business outcome, cost limit and stop rule before launch. Use a comparable holdout or randomized assignment when feasible, avoid simultaneous untracked changes, account for seasonality and capacity, and distinguish attribution from incrementality. If a small pilot cannot resolve the effect, report uncertainty rather than declaring victory from engagement. No unverified experimental citation is claimed here.
