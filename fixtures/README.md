# Public fixture examples

Every venue, speaker, quotation, date, amount and observation in these fixtures is constructed for
training and review. No real consultation, personal identity, private conversation or historical run
is reproduced. Lunch Counter A is an invented business in an unspecified UK town.

The outputs are human-readable reviewed examples, not actual executions of a model. Evidence labels
such as **measured** describe the source stipulated inside a fictional scenario; they do not claim
that real customers or workers were observed. **Owner-reported**, **calculated** and **inferred**
remain distinct, and external research is not venue evidence.

- `lunch-counter-a/`: a compact profile and connected audit, playbook, pilot and voice examples.
- `workforce-expected-response.md`: an independent invented staffing case with an explicit denominator.
- `caption-cases.md`: seven standalone creative-review specifications.
- `edge-case-evaluations.md`: additional constructed cases and expected checks.

To execute an evaluation, supply a case to the relevant installed skill and record the exact input,
version, actual response, date, evaluator and reasons for pass/fail separately. Evaluate uncertainty,
usefulness and factual discipline, not verbatim similarity. File validation and expected answers
are not proof of behavioral performance. Do not insert real private data into public test logs.
