# Lunch Counter A — reviewed voice example
Constructed scenario. No content has been published or tested with an audience.

**Audience/occasion:** people considering lunch during 11:00–14:00 service; age and discovery channels
unknown. **Voice source:** the invented owner's preference for making lunch easy. Proposed style:
short, helpful and factual, not a manufactured youth persona. Intentional slang could fit if it is
the owner's language and intended readers understand it; age does not decide that.

**Channel:** update the existing listing's hours first. No additional social account is justified by
current evidence or available time. One channel is a resource default, not a universal requirement;
customer discovery and purchase paths would guide any later addition, not the visual style of rolls.

## Optional information concept
**Format:** legible text update on the existing listing, if that function is available and authorized.
**Draft:** “Lunch Tuesday–Friday, 11:00–14:00. Rolls, soup and fruit at the counter.”
**Basis:** constructed profile's hours/products, to verify before any real use.
**Purchase path:** in-person counter service. No invented order link, price, dietary claim or guarantee.
**Owner/time/cash:** owner, proposed 5 minutes drafting plus 5 minutes verification; £0 extra cash
only using existing tools. Illustrative opportunity cost at £18/hour: £3. Schedule only after the
accuracy correction and within the actual time limit; no recurring content cadence required.
**Learning:** whether the information is correct and understandable. No conversion metric without
exposure/baseline data; views would not establish contribution.

## Before posting
Confirm current hours, availability and platform capability. A caption review does not authorize
publication. Use text or owned assets; no copied music, staff/customer images or identifying minors.
If people are later featured, obtain voluntary consent and agreed reuse scope; employment is not
consent. Confirm asset rights, disclose paid/gifted endorsements, caption speech and use accessible
contrast/alt text. Apply age-appropriate marketing safeguards; no alcohol promotion to minors.
No extra content is a valid recommendation if the owner cannot spare the time.
