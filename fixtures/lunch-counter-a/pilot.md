# Lunch Counter A — collection-label pilot example
All inputs are constructed. Reviewed design, not an executed model test or business pilot.
question: feasibility and descriptive adoption/wayfinding, not causal revenue impact
window: provisional; eight normal Tuesday–Friday services after the listing correction is verified
owner: owner, subject to the 20-minute preparation limit and no service disruption

## Before launch
Confirm label placement, £2 cash cap, 10-minute setup and 2-minute-per-service recording limit from
the playbook. If the earlier hours task uses the available preparation slot, wait for another slot.
No calendar commitment is invented. Gather a short fresh baseline after the hours correction only
if a tally is proportionate; otherwise run a feasibility-only check without claiming an impact rate.

Existing two-day context is 5/39 purchasing parties asking where to collect. Baseline variation and
lost demand are unknown. No power or detectable-effect claim follows from 39 observations.

## Comparison and records
Paper aggregate per service: purchasing parties, parties asking about collection, whether the card
was present/visible, tally minutes and access/queue problems. Count each party once per outcome,
not each repeated question. Do not guess ages or collect names/photos. Retain aggregates for the
review, then discard unnecessary working notes. No new analytics tool assumed.

Compare like weekdays descriptively only if a fresh baseline exists. Log hours, staffing, weather,
local events and any layout/menu changes. Different customer mixes and the corrected listing can
confound before/after results. No concurrent control is specified, so changes cannot be attributed
causally to the label. New orders or a lower question count are not proof of incremental profit.

## Decision after eight services
- **Keep for operational usefulness:** placement is accessible, staff find it helpful, and cash/time
  caps hold. This is not a claim of proven sales or question-rate impact.
- **Tweak:** one observed placement problem is safely repairable within the remaining cap; record
  the revision as a new version, rather than silently pooling its observations.
- **Stop:** obstruction, meaningful service burden, no practical usefulness or costs exceed limits.
- **Inconclusive:** insufficient comparable exposure or conflicting feedback. No automatic extension;
  make no further change unless a bounded follow-up would inform a worthwhile decision.

At the scenario's illustrative £18/hour rate, time costs are £3 setup + £4.80 recurring; with £2
materials, maximum planned resource cost is £9.80. No wage expense is assumed, but actual paid cover
would be additional and requires recosting. Contribution and displacement remain unknown; do not
claim payback. Remove the card to reverse. Outcome: not run; no result to write as measured success.
