## Decision needs
1. Correct the confirmed listing error, within authorization, without waiting for an experiment.
2. Consider a modest collection-label feasibility test if the owner wants it. Questions might reflect
   first visits, the layout or unclear staff instructions; a label may not resolve them.
3. Leave broader acquisition and product changes alone pending a specific need and feasible evidence.

**Calculated:** (2 + 3) / (18 + 21) = 5/39 purchasing parties, about 12.8%, asked about collection.
Two days are not a stable baseline, and the denominator excludes people who never purchased.
**Inferred:** a clearer collection marker might reduce confusion. It might also obstruct circulation.
An unchanged question rate after comparable exposure would weaken that hypothesis; other explanations
and a tiny sample could still leave the result inconclusive.

## Research applicability and limits
P03 concerns discovery and P05/P07 transaction paths, but no external statistic establishes local
conversion or proves this label will help. P02 may inform takeaway checks if an issue emerges.
P12 applies proportionally to the small seating area, not an obligatory event. P11 alcohol substitution
is inapplicable; nonalcoholic drink suitability can still matter. P14 flavor is not excluded because
there is no alcohol. Research elsewhere is a question source, not a venue diagnosis.
