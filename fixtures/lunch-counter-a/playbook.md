# Lunch Counter A — reviewed playbook example
Constructed scenario, 2026-11-11. Cash ceiling £60; owner has up to 20 minutes after Thursday service.
No action here has been executed or authorizes external changes.

## Now: correct the listing
**Finding:** D1 supported accuracy concern; P03 is context, not a sales forecast.
**Action/owner:** owner verifies 14:00 close and updates the existing listing when authorized.
**Costs:** constructed estimate £0 additional cash, 10 minutes setup and 5 minutes verification;
recurring checks only when hours change. At an illustrative £18/hour opportunity-cost rate, setup
and verification use £4.50 of time. No new subscription or displaced service work.
**Measure:** inspect the published closing time. Sales baseline is unknown; no uplift target needed.
**Decision/reversal:** keep accurate hours. Correct any further error; never restore false hours for
a test. Log the date because it can confound later demand comparisons.

## Next, optional: collection label
**Finding/hypothesis:** D3 narrow concern, P05/P07 context. A visible “Collect here” card may answer
some wayfinding questions; layout or instructions may matter more.
**Action/owner:** only after the hours task, owner checks placement does not obstruct access and
makes one card. No product, price or staffing change. Do nothing if a safe location is unavailable.
**Costs:** constructed planning cap £2 materials; 10 minutes setup plus 2 minutes per service for
checking/tallying. At £18/hour, setup time is £3 and eight services' recurring time is £4.80.
Total trial resource cost would be at most £9.80: £2 + £3 + £4.80, excluding earlier hours correction.
Owner must confirm these time assumptions fit the actual workload; service work is not free.
**Economics:** no expected sales or margin gain asserted. Product contribution is unknown. This
information aid changes no price, but blocked space or tallying can displace service and purchases.
**Measure/baseline:** purchasing parties asking where to collect / all purchasing parties, using
existing paper tally; two constructed prior days are weak descriptive context, not a control.
**Window:** eight services for feasibility; causal business impact not established by this design.
**Guardrails:** no blocked access, identifiable customer records, extra queueing or spending over cap.
**Decision:** keep as a low-burden aid only if usable and affordable; revise placement for a visible
problem; stop for harm; inconclusive if exposure or burden cannot be recorded reliably.
**Reversal:** remove the card. Material/time costs are sunk; no recurring obligation or customer promise.

No promotion, ordering software, rewards or additional content is required. No change beyond accurate
hours is a valid outcome. The pilot describes a possible test, not a mandatory metric program.
